Colori della Catastrofe

Color as a temporal process. Two chromatic experiences, four animated fields exploring catastrophe through pure abstraction.

Concept

Colori della Catastrofe is a project that investigates color as the sole expressive element — stripped of figures, symbols, and narrative. Each animated field treats color as a living process: a phenomenon that unfolds in time, reacting to the format it inhabits.

Starting from a vocabulary of catastrophe, two words were selected as conceptual anchors — not to be illustrated, but to be translated into the temporal behavior of color. The result is four animated tableaux in which chromatic instability, tension, and collapse become the subject itself.

Dissolution

First chromatic experience

Dissolution as the slow erosion of chromatic identity. The color doesn't disappear suddenly — it loses cohesion over time, bleeding into surrounding tones until form and boundary cease to exist. In the horizontal format, the dissolution extends laterally, evoking a horizon line that gradually disintegrates. In the vertical format, the same process becomes gravitational — color descends, drains, and empties the field from within.

Intermittence

Second chromatic experience

Intermittence as a system on the verge of failure. The color pulses, flickers, and stutters — a signal that keeps attempting to persist but is caught in a cycle of disruption. The horizontal format frames the intermittence as a scanning line, a broadcast struggling to maintain its frequency. The vertical format compresses the pulsation into a narrow column of instability, where each flash feels closer to the final blackout.

Process

The two colors were treated as entirely distinct perceptual experiences, not as variations of the same palette. Each chromatic field was developed to respond specifically to its format: the horizontal frame and the vertical frame become different perceptual contexts that alter the way color moves, accumulates, and collapses.

No figurative elements, symbols, or recognizable shapes were used. Texture, grain, and noise serve exclusively as modifiers of chromatic behavior — tools to amplify the sense of instability, entropy, and irreversible transformation that defines catastrophe.
