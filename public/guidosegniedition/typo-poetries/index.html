Typo / Poetries

RUMORE

Concept

RUMORE is a kinetic and auditory exploration of language, shape, and time. Derived from the vocabulary of catastrophe, the word "RUMORE" (Noise) is brought to life through kinetic typography. The objective is not to represent the word literally, but to make it happen visually in time.

The typography trembles, fractures, and dissolves, creating a synesthetic feedback loop where text and sound merge and amplify one another. It is a poetic investigation of language in its physical and auditory dimension, where letters cease to be static signs and become vibration and event.

RUMORE

Typographic Animation & Sound

The choice of the word RUMORE (Noise) and the typographic animation stem from a desire to visually translate acoustic vibration and signal interference. The letters break apart and tremble in sync with a harsh soundscape, merging typography and sound into a single wave of interference.

Process

The workflow started in Figma, utilizing frame-by-frame manipulation of letterforms, weight, position, and opacity. Through Smart Animate, these frames were sequenced to form a coherent typographic movement that mimics noise and frequency interference.

The kinetic typography is paired with sound to trigger a synesthetic experience where word and sound are fused, transforming language from a conveyor of meaning into a physical, moving texture.
